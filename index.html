Peter Merholz

Merholz says the legibility problem in design never actually resolves

In a new instalment building on his budget argument, Peter Merholz writes that organizations value predictability so they can plan and budget, and predictability requires determinacy, where the nature of work is understood ahead of time, which in turn defines legibility, the qualities of work that can be seen, appreciated, valued, and accounted for . He adds that in the overwhelming majority of organizations, a design leader's work in this regard is never done .

So whatreorganising the design function won't make the legibility problem disappear once and for all, it just resets the negotiation, so the group design function needs a permanent operating habit for translating design work into planning language rather than a one-off fix.

The Pragmatic Engineer

Charity Majors: unread AI code is fine if telemetry does the reviewing

In a fuller writeup of her Pragmatic Engineer conversation, Charity Majors argues the honest question is no longer whether engineers will ship code they have not read, but when, and what must be true for that to become acceptable , with observability replacing manual code review as the safety net.

So whatfor a design function increasingly shipping AI-generated interface and personalisation logic, this reframes the QA question from "who reviewed this" to "what telemetry would catch it going wrong," which has direct implications for how research and evaluation practice needs to be redesigned.

ICO's children's-data crackdown shows where profiling enforcement is heading

Under the UK's phased data reforms, the ICO has reviewed 34 social media and video-sharing platforms focusing on default privacy and geolocation settings, profiling for targeted advertising, and age assurance, and has already fined two platforms a combined total of over £14.7 million for shortcomings in handling children's data .

So whatthe enforcement pattern (default settings, profiling for advertising, age assurance) is a preview of where scrutiny of any consumer-facing personalisation engine is likely to go next, loyalty and app personalisation included.
